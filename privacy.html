Effective 1 April 2026 · Updated 6 May 2026

1. Introduction

Tinkl ("the App") is a social planning application operated by Daniel Buttacavoli, a sole trader based in Australia ("we", "us", "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use the Tinkl mobile application and related services. It applies to all users of Tinkl, regardless of location.

We are committed to complying with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). By creating an account and using Tinkl, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2.3 Information Collected With Your Permission

About location data: When you create a Shake, your device's GPS coordinates are used locally to determine a place name (e.g., "Hyde Park, Sydney"). Only the place name and suburb are sent to our servers. Location access is never used in the background.

When you select a location for a calendar event via search, the place name, formatted address, and geographic coordinates are stored to enable accurate directions and ride-sharing links. Coordinates are never displayed to users.

2.4 Information Processed by AI Features

When you use Tinkl's optional AI features ("Make it an event" suggestions in chat, and the "Quick draft" prompt at the top of Create Event), the natural-language text you tap or type is sent to OpenAI for parsing into a draft event. This processing happens only when you tap an AI suggestion or the Generate button — it is never automatic and never includes anyone else's messages.

Information When Collected Purpose
Chat message text (the single tapped message)When you tap an "AI draft" suggestion in chatGenerate a structured event draft for your review
Free-form prompt textWhen you tap "Generate" in the AI quick-draft inputGenerate a structured event draft for your review
Calendar timezone identifierSent alongside both flowsResolve relative phrases like "tomorrow" correctly

We log per-user metadata (timestamp, success/failure, latency) in our database for rate-limiting and cost monitoring. We do not persist the message or prompt text on our servers.

The Tinkl iMessage Extension and Siri "Create a Tinkl event" command use the same OpenAI integration described above. The text you type or speak is sent to OpenAI for parsing only when you tap Generate (or run the Siri command); your spoken or typed input is not sent automatically.

2.5 Mentions and Year-End Wrapped

@mentions: When you type @<name> in a calendar chat, the mentioned member receives a notification with your message. We do not share your messages outside the calendar's membership.

Year-end Wrapped: Once per year (December 1) we generate an in-app summary of each calendar's activity (event counts, photo stickers collected, top contributors) using data already in your account. Wrapped is visible only to members of the relevant calendar and is computed on demand each time you open it. We log a small record of share actions (no message content) for product analytics.

2.6 Local Caching on Your Device

Some app data is cached locally on your device using encrypted local storage to enable offline access and faster startup. This cached data never leaves your device.

2.7 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Background location data or continuous GPS tracking
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Credit card numbers or bank login credentials (we only store payment receiving details you provide for cost-splitting)
  • Health or fitness data
  • Browsing or search history outside the App
  • Advertising identifiers or device IDs for tracking
  • Biometric data

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in the Sydney, Australia (ap-southeast-2) region. File uploads (profile photos, calendar cover images, stickers, memory photos) are stored in Supabase Storage in the same region.

5.2 Admin Access to User Content

As the operator of Tinkl, we have technical access to all data stored on our servers, including user-uploaded photos, messages, and profile information. This is inherent to operating a database-backed service and is consistent with industry practice.

Our commitments regarding admin access:

  • No routine browsing: We do not browse, view, or access user-uploaded photos, messages, or personal content as part of normal operations. There is no admin interface for viewing user photos.
  • Access only when necessary: We will only access user content to respond to reported abuse or safety concerns, comply with valid legal requests, investigate technical issues, or at your explicit request.
  • Audit logging: All administrative access to user content is logged in an audit trail, including who accessed what, when, and why.
  • No sharing: User content accessed during investigations is never shared externally except as required by law.

5.3 Security Measures

We implement the following security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Encryption at rest: All data at rest is encrypted by Supabase's infrastructure.
  • Secure token storage: Authentication tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain (or Android equivalent), not in plaintext.
  • Database access control: Every database table has Row Level Security (RLS) enabled, ensuring users can only access data they are authorised to see.
  • API key security: Third-party API keys are stored server-side and never shipped in the app binary.

5.4 Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all your data is permanently deleted (see Section 7). We do not retain copies of deleted data.

6. Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

6.1 Right of Access (APP 12)

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you. Most of your information is directly accessible within the App (profile, calendars, messages, friends). For a formal access request, contact us at support@tinkl.au.

6.2 Right of Correction (APP 13)

You may update or correct your personal information at any time through the App:

  • Display name and bio: Settings > Edit Profile
  • Profile photo: Settings > Edit Profile
  • Password: Settings > Change Password

If you believe any information we hold is inaccurate and you cannot correct it through the App, contact us at support@tinkl.au.

6.3 Right of Deletion

You may permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time through the App (see Section 7).

6.4 Right to Complain

If you believe we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles or otherwise mishandled your personal information, you may:

  1. Contact us first: Email support@tinkl.au with details of your concern. We will investigate and respond within 30 days.
  2. Complain to the OAIC: If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au or by calling 1300 363 992.

7. Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account from within the App:

Settings > Delete Account

When you delete your account, the following is immediately and permanently removed from our servers:

  • Your authentication credentials (email, password)
  • Your profile (display name, bio, profile photo)
  • All calendars you own
  • All your calendar memberships
  • All messages you have sent
  • All event RSVPs and availability submissions
  • All Shakes (beacons) you have created
  • All your friendships and friend requests
  • All your notifications and push tokens
  • Your badges, titles, and stickers
  • Your blocked user records and notification preferences
  • All uploaded files (profile photos, calendar cover images, memory photos)

Deletion is immediate and irreversible. There is no grace period or recovery option.

8. Children's Privacy

Tinkl is intended for users aged 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has created a Tinkl account or provided personal information to us, please contact us at support@tinkl.au. We will promptly delete the child's account and all associated information.

If you are aged between 13 and 18, we encourage you to discuss your use of Tinkl with a parent or guardian.
