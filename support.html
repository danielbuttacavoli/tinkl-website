Report abuse or a safety concern

Tinkl has zero tolerance for harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content, content involving minors, and anything illegal (Terms, section 4A).

  • Block someone: open their profile and tap Block. Blocking ends your friendship, and they can't send you friend requests, Shakes or invites.
  • Report it to us: email support@tinkl.au with the person's name, the calendar if it was in a chat, what happened and, if you can, a screenshot.
  • What happens next: we review every report within 24 hours. If content breaks our terms we remove it and suspend or permanently remove the account.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 000 first.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings > Delete Account and confirm.

  • Deletion is immediate and permanent. There's no recovery.
  • It removes your profile, calendars you own, your messages, events, Shakes, friendships, photos and everything else listed in our Privacy Policy, section 7.
  • Can't get into the app? Email support@tinkl.au from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it for you.

Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?. Open the reset email on your iPhone, so the link opens in Tinkl.

I'm not getting notifications

Check iPhone Settings > Notifications > Tinkl is on, then in Tinkl check Settings > Notification Preferences (muted types, quiet hours, muted friends and calendars). Send Test Push on that screen checks the whole path.

The iMessage app or Siri says to open Tinkl

Open Tinkl once while signed in. That refreshes the session the iMessage app and Siri use.

A shared event link says "Page not found" or won't open

Make sure Tinkl is installed and you're signed in, then tap the link again. If you're not a member of that calendar yet, you'll see a preview and can ask to join.

How do I change my name, photo or birthday?

In Tinkl, go to Settings > Edit Profile.

Is Tinkl on Android?

Not yet. Tinkl is launching on iPhone first, and Android is on the way.
