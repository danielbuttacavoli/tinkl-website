Common questions

Everything you're
wondering.

Is Tinkl really free?

Yes, completely. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads. Tinkl is free to download and free to use.

What's a Shake?

A Shake is a quick, spontaneous invite. Pick some friends, write a message like "Coffee in 20?", and everyone gets notified instantly. They reply with one tap — "I'm in" or "Maybe" — and you see who's joining in real time.

How is this different from a group chat?

Group chats mix memes, banter, and logistics into one noisy thread. Tinkl separates signal from noise. See who's free at a glance, send targeted invites to the right people, and plan bigger events with a shared calendar. No more scrolling through 80 messages to find out if anyone's around.

Can I use Tinkl with just a few friends?

Absolutely. Tinkl works great with any size group — from 2 close friends to a larger crew. You don't need a huge network to get value out of it.

Is my data private?

Very. Your information is only shared with friends you've accepted. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't track you. All data is stored in Australia. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

What platforms is Tinkl on?

Tinkl is available on both iOS and Android.
